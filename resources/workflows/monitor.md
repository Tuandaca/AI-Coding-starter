# Monitor Workflow

## Purpose
Establish comprehensive observability for production systems.

## Steps

1. **Instrumentation**
   - OpenTelemetry integration
   - Structured logging (JSON)
   - Distributed tracing
   - Custom business metrics
   - Health check endpoints

2. **Dashboards**
   - Golden signals (RED/USE)
   - Business KPI overview
   - Service-specific panels
   - On-call runbook links

3. **Alerting**
   - SLO-based alerts
   - Multi-window burn rate
   - Tiered notification (page/ticket)
   - Alert grouping/deduplication
   - Runbook links in alerts

4. **Log Management**
   - Centralized logging
   - Retention policies
   - Query optimization
   - PII scrubbing
   - Cost optimization

5. **Incident Response**
   - On-call rotation
   - Escalation policies
   - War room procedures
   - Post-incident reviews
   - Action item tracking

## Outputs
- Full observability stack
- Actionable alerts
- Incident response capability
- Performance insights
- Business visibility
